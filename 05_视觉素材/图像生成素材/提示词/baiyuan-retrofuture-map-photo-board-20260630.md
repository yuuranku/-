# Baiyuan Retrofuture Map Photo Board

16:9 main visual concept art for an alternate-history Antarctic under-ice horror world, retrofuturist cold war archival style.

Core composition:
- A large stylized Antarctica map fills the background, pale ice-gray on worn off-white paper.
- The map is clean and simplified, not geographically over-detailed.
- Several small station markers are placed across the map: black dots, red warning dots, muted blue survey dots.
- Thin leader lines extend from 4 or 5 selected station markers to small inset photographs around the map.
- The inset photographs must look like degraded 1940s-1960s black-and-white film prints: grainy, blurred, low contrast, uneven chemical development, dust, scratches, slight halation, archival rephotography.
- Photo content should show vague polar stations, borehole towers, snow vehicles, distant antenna masts, and one ambiguous dark under-ice opening. Keep them atmospheric and indistinct, never modern crisp photos.

Style:
- Retrofuturist cold war graphic design, mid-century scientific dossier, constructivist geometry, alternate-history grand strategy flat visual language.
- Not a UI screenshot, not an aviation chart, not a dense infographic.
- Strong visual hierarchy, lots of breathing room, clean margins.
- Palette: off-white, ice gray, charcoal black, warning red, muted cyan-blue, small aged yellow.
- Red diagonal hatching appears only in one restricted zone on the map.
- Subtle paper texture, analog print grain, slight misregistration.

Typography:
- Sparse English only, used as design accent.
- Suggested text: "WHITE ABYSS", "1964", "RESTRICTED POLAR FILE", "STATION RECORDS DO NOT CLOSE".
- Text should be small, clean, modernist, integrated into the layout. No paragraphs.

Mood:
- Narrative but restrained: a viewer should feel this is a classified cold war wall-board showing stations connected to photographic evidence.
- The horror is in the mismatch between map, stations, and photographs, not monsters.
- No phone UI, no watermark, no logos, no readable real-world brand marks, no copied reference image content.
