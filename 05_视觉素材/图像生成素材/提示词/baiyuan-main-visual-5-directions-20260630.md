# 白渊纪事主视觉 16:9 - 五个方向

## 视觉哲学：极地档案构成主义

以国家机器试图整理未知为核心视觉逻辑。画面不是普通海报，而像一份被设计到极致的冷战密档：地图、剖面、航图、编号、红蓝黄标记和硬边几何互相叠压。文字只作为坐标、封条和档案编号出现，真正的信息由空间结构、线条密度、色块冲突和缺失区域传达。

画面需要有上世纪中叶资料感，但执行应极其现代、精密、干净。构成主义的斜线、圆点和几何张力要服务于“闭合失败”的主题：队伍、路线、冰层、国家边界和档案编号都试图闭合，却在某处断开。每一处线框、虚线、剖面和编号都应像经过长时间手工排版、校准和打磨。

色彩限制在黑、白、冰灰、深炭、警戒红、档案蓝、少量陈旧黄。红色代表争议区、错误归队、国家级命令；蓝色代表科考、测线、冰层和无线电；黄色只作为异常标记或索引点。不要做赛博霓虹，不要做游戏截图，不要做普通科幻概念图。

主体应该传达“南极冰面之下存在类远古生态系统”：冰盖、井口、斜井、地下古生态层、黑湖、蕨状低林、盲甲鱼、长枝兽或古两栖样剪影可以以抽象/符号方式出现。保持克制，不要怪物乐园。整体要像一张被封存的主视觉图，能作为世界观封面、短片 KV、档案叙事首图。

## Direction 4: White Archival Cover
16:9 high-end editorial cover, mostly white and pale gray paper, constructivist abstract composition. Oversized black irregular continent/ice silhouette broken into rectangular crop windows, each window reveals a different layer: surface station, slanted borehole, fern-like ancient under-ice world, black lake, old bone strata, national command memo. Thin lines radiate from one yellow point to many mismatched evidence boxes. Red dashed triangle marks "unclosed zone"; blue small symbols mark survey teams. Text is very sparse and elegant: "白渊纪事", "WHITE ABYSS CHRONICLES", "1964". Inspired by archival investigation posters and mid-century modern technical publishing, refined flat design, tactile paper texture, precise margins, no explanatory paragraphs, no stock-photo realism.

## Direction 5: Public Crisis Collage
16:9 classified crisis-board main visual. A cold war public crisis collage: Antarctic map fragments, radio log strips, hospital chart boxes, family notification forms, aircraft route plate, and a central under-ice cutaway all arranged in hard geometric constructivist layout. The visual anchor is a red stamped route that should close but does not; two blue route versions disagree; yellow dots pin contradictory evidence. Include abstract silhouettes of primitive under-ice creatures only as black cutout traces, not monsters. Sparse labels: "短波点名事件", "失踪报告", "南极公约监督议定书", "1962-1964". Palette black, white, gray, red, blue, old yellow. Looks like a master-crafted alternate-history cold war dossier cover, flat graphic, systematic, ominous, no gore, no propaganda logos, no phone UI, no watermark.
