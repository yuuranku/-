# Baiyuan Setting-Based Main Visual V2

16:9 main visual key art for "WHITE ABYSS CHRONICLES", an alternate-history cold war Antarctic under-ice horror world.

The design must be based on this specific setting:
- Year: 1964.
- Central Antarctica hides the White Abyss below the ice cap.
- Surface stations and boreholes are public-facing scientific infrastructure.
- Under the ice is a primitive ancient ecosystem: root-plate forest, fern-like low growth, black lakes, old bone deposits, blind armored fish, distant branch-like animal silhouettes.
- The core anomaly is "failure to close": station records, return records, route lines, rescue status, and photographic evidence contradict each other.
- The visual should feel like a cold war classified wall-board, not a normal map.

Composition:
- Background: a large simplified Antarctica map, pale ice-gray, clean but recognizable, occupying the center-left.
- From the central plateau, one black vertical borehole/shaft cuts down through the map into a dark under-ice cross-section band along the bottom third.
- In the under-ice band, show only subtle silhouettes of ancient root-plate forest and black lake shapes, blurred and half hidden, not monster art.
- Use 3 old black-and-white film photo insets, not many:
  1. a surface polar station with antennas,
  2. a borehole tower or lift cage descending into ice,
  3. an under-ice dark lake / primitive forest trace, almost unreadable.
- All photographs must look like degraded 1940s-1960s film: grain, blur, low contrast, dust, scratches, uneven development, archival rephotography, no modern crispness.
- Thin leader lines connect selected station dots on the map to the three photos.
- The three leader lines should not perfectly agree; one line should visibly miss its station dot or split into two close alternatives.
- One restrained red diagonal restricted zone around the central plateau, plus a few muted blue survey dots.

Design style:
- Retrofuturist cold war graphic design, constructivist layout, mid-century scientific dossier, severe but elegant.
- Inspired by old classified polar files, not by modern UI screenshots.
- Clean hierarchy, strong margins, not cluttered, not too many labels.
- Palette: off-white paper, ice gray map, charcoal black, warning red, muted cyan-blue, tiny aged yellow.
- Subtle analog paper grain and print misregistration.

Typography:
- English only, sparse and well placed.
- Text elements: "WHITE ABYSS", "1964", "WEST LINE INCIDENT", "RETURN STATUS: INVALID".
- No paragraphs, no tiny unreadable data blocks, no dense infographics.

Avoid:
- Do not copy any reference image content.
- Do not create a generic Antarctic expedition poster.
- Do not use modern UI, aviation chart panels, phone screenshots, web interface, clean digital photos, logos, watermarks.
- Do not overcrowd with many station photos or labels.
- Do not show explicit monsters or action scenes.
