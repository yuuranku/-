# White Abyss archival photo self-narrations

01. I was taken before the hole had a proper name. The men beside the drill look still, but the snow has already swallowed half the machinery.

02. I caught the first green shapes under the ice, if they were green at all. The flash burned the cave white, and everything beyond it kept breathing in the dark.

03. I was not meant to be clear. Someone turned, someone shouted, and whatever crossed the water left only a wet line where a body should have been.

04. I lay on a lab table for three hours while they argued over what counted as an animal. By the time they filed me, the specimen had already lost one limb and two names.

05. I am the last frame before the tunnel record breaks. Three men are running toward me, but the fourth shape in the back is the one no roster could keep.

06. No one is in the room, or that is what the report says. The cups, coats, and radio logs disagree in small, patient ways.

07. I was filed as an ordinary station portrait. Look long enough and the same man stands twice, older in one place and colder in the other.

08. I remember the evacuation badly. Snow crossed the lens, engines dragged the frame sideways, and the crates mattered more than the people carrying them.

09. I was exposed for too long in front of the ice wall. The instruments stayed sharp enough, but the figures inside the ice refused to become either reflection or evidence.

10. I was taken from a moving vehicle, so nobody believed me at first. The lights caught legs, backs, and breath, then the plain took them back.
