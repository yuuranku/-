# Baiyuan Minimal Concept Visuals - 5 Directions

Shared constraints for all images:
- 16:9 main visual concept art, minimal and clean.
- Do not copy or include the reference images' specific contents.
- Do not make dense infographics, maps, UI screenshots, aviation plates, or busy dossier boards.
- No literal monster scene, no crowded labels, no excessive lore objects.
- Use English text only if useful, sparse and typographic: 1-4 short words maximum.
- Visual language: constructivism, mid-century cold war poster design, flat graphic abstraction, severe linework, large negative space.
- Palette: black, off-white, ice gray, warning red, muted blue, small aged yellow accent.
- Mood: Antarctic secrecy, under-ice absence, national memory distortion, cold archival horror.
- If any photographic element appears, it must look like degraded 1940s-1960s black-and-white film: blurred, grainy, low contrast, uneven development, archival rephotography, slight halation, dust and scratches. No clean modern screenshots, no phone UI, no crisp digital photo panels.

## Direction 1 - The Cut
A minimal 16:9 constructivist concept poster. Vast off-white ice field background, one huge black vertical slit cutting downward from the top center into negative space, like an impossible borehole through the Antarctic ice. At the bottom of the slit, a barely visible degraded black-and-white film texture suggests something under the ice, blurred and grainy, not literal. A single thin red diagonal line crosses the slit, one tiny blue survey dot near the edge, and a small yellow index point. Sparse English text in a small modernist sans-serif: "ABYSS / 1964". Strong asymmetry, extreme negative space, crisp vector-like edges, subtle paper grain, mid-century cold war graphic design, ominous but restrained.

## Direction 2 - Unclosed Route
A minimal 16:9 flat graphic concept image. Pale gray background with one incomplete circular route line, almost closing but broken by a black void at the lower third. A small red triangle interrupts the route, two tiny blue line segments disagree with each other. No map details, no labels except tiny text: "RETURN: INVALID". Constructivist composition, cold bureaucratic horror, TNO-like alternate-history flat design but original, precise linework, lots of empty space, no clutter.

## Direction 3 - State Memory
A minimal 16:9 cold war concept poster. Three large abstract rectangular blocks in black, gray, and muted red arranged like state documents or national flags stripped of symbols. Thin white lines connect them to a single black under-ice shape below, suggesting a hidden system influencing state memory. One short English label: "MEMORY FILE". Very clean, severe, geometric, 1960s institutional design, constructivist balance, no figures, no monsters, no dense information.

## Direction 4 - Under-Ice Forest Trace
A minimal 16:9 abstract visual. White ice plane on top, black underground band below. Inside the black band, only a few thin pale lines suggest primitive root-plates or ancient under-ice plant silhouettes, almost like scratches on damaged black-and-white film. A red vertical measuring line descends from the surface but stops before touching the roots. Small text: "BELOW THE ICE". Quiet, spare, elegant, archival, mid-century scientific cover design, flat design with subtle grain.

## Direction 5 - Antarctic Convention Oversight Protocol
A minimal 16:9 graphic poster. Dark charcoal background, one long horizontal white line across the center, broken into three misaligned segments. A small red stamp-like rectangle sits over the break, and a small blue dot appears where the line should have closed. No maps, no creatures, no dense UI. Sparse English text: "ACOA / OVERSIGHT PROTOCOL". Strong constructivist geometry, cold war secrecy, severe composition, premium editorial poster, clean margins.
