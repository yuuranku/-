【English】The mission was dispatched as routine ecological sampling, with additional survey maintenance.

【English】The dispatch order listed three items: photograph and collect black conifer root-plate and surface frost-crystal samples; recover the HZ-6C air filter; update the return markers.

【English】Thomas E. Mallory, photographer, BAS civilian imaging technician. Status: survived.

【中文】M29C Weasel 停放于 HZ-6A 车辆点，不进入低林区域。
【English】The M29C Weasel was parked at the HZ-6A vehicle point and did not enter the low forest area.

【English】Mallory carried two cameras.

【中文】Rolleiflex Automat MX 使用 Kodak Plus-X Pan 120 胶卷，用于静态样本和根板记录。
【English】The Rolleiflex Automat MX used Kodak Plus-X Pan 120 film for static sample and root-plate records.

【中文】Nikon S 35 毫米旁轴相机装载 Kodak Super-XX Panchromatic 胶卷，配 Nikkor-S.C 5cm f/1.4 与 Nikkor-Q.C 13.5cm f/3.5 镜头，用于行进、人员和突发情况记录。
【English】The Nikon S 35mm rangefinder was loaded with Kodak Super-XX Panchromatic film and fitted with Nikkor-S.C 5cm f/1.4 and Nikkor-Q.C 13.5cm f/3.5 lenses for movement, personnel, and unexpected-event records.

【中文】随身另有 Weston Master III 测光表、Graflex 闪光灯支架、Sylvania M3 闪光灯泡二十四枚及备用胶卷。
【English】He also carried a Weston Master III light meter, a Graflex flash bracket, twenty-four Sylvania M3 flashbulbs, and spare film.


【中文】0738，小队乘 M29C Weasel 从地平线站二号东门出发，沿通风车道前往 HZ-6A。
【English】At 0738, the team left Horizon Station's East Gate Two in the M29C Weasel and followed the ventilation road toward HZ-6A.

【中文】0925，抵达 HZ-6A。车辆停放于标志桩外侧，基恩接入线缆中继箱。马洛里使用 Rolleiflex 拍摄六张根板与表层霜晶照片。
【English】At 0925, they reached HZ-6A. The vehicle was parked outside the marker post, Keene connected to the cable relay box, and Mallory used the Rolleiflex to take six photographs of root plates and surface frost crystals.

【中文】1110，克莱恩在样本点北侧采集白壳碎片与地表霜晶样品。气体检测未见异常。
【English】At 1110, Klein collected white-shell fragments and surface frost-crystal samples north of the sample point. Gas detection showed no abnormality.

【中文】1240，午餐暂停。基恩报告林缘方向存在重复啄击声，暂按“静默鸟”活动记录。马洛里使用 Nikon S 拍摄两张正常行进照片。
【English】At 1240, the team paused for lunch. Keene reported repeated pecking sounds from the forest edge, temporarily logged as "silent bird" activity. Mallory took two normal movement photographs with the Nikon S.

【中文】1518，抵达 HZ-6C。克莱恩取下空气滤芯盒并装入样本箱。现场笔记记录树脂气味、湿树皮，以及标记点附近不寻常的低位拖痕。
【English】At 1518, they reached HZ-6C. Klein removed the air-filter box and placed it in the sample case. Field notes recorded a resin smell, wet bark, and unusual low drag marks near the marker point.

【中文】1712，冰顶漫射光与采样标灯亮度同时下降，速度快于预期。拉斯克下令原路返回 HZ-6A。
【English】At 1712, the diffuse light from the ice cap and the sample marker lamp dimmed at the same time, faster than expected. Rusk ordered a return by the original route to HZ-6A.

【中文】1819，基恩报告啄击声停止。马洛里后述称，此时他第一次在无人命令的情况下举起相机。
【English】At 1819, Keene reported that the pecking sounds had stopped. Mallory later stated that this was the first time he raised the camera without being ordered to do so.

【中文】1822，发生第一次非计划闪光拍摄。马洛里认为该帧记录了拉斯克中尉转向林缘的瞬间。
【English】At 1822, the first unplanned flash exposure occurred. Mallory believed the frame captured Lieutenant Rusk turning toward the forest edge.

【中文】1824 至 1828，基恩尝试经 HZ-6A 中继回报返程延迟。地平线站仅收到断续语音：“HZ-6 小队正在检查行进区域东侧疑似移动目标。”该句不视为正式判断或求救报告。
【English】From 1824 to 1828, Keene attempted to report the delayed return through the HZ-6A relay. Horizon Station received only a broken transmission: "HZ-6 team is checking a suspected moving target east of the movement area." This statement is not treated as an official assessment or distress report.

【中文】1829，推定发生未分类动物接触。马洛里在第一次询问中称目标“贴着树根出来”。从目标抬起前端到小队分散之间，马洛里的连续记忆中断。
【English】At 1829, unclassified animal contact is presumed to have occurred. In his first questioning, Mallory said the target "came out against the tree roots." From the target raising its front end to the team scattering, Mallory's continuous memory breaks off.

【中文】1831 至 1836，小队撤退并散开。底片顺序显示马洛里在奔跑、摔倒或受到冲撞后仍多次按下 Nikon S 快门。闪光灯并非每次触发。万斯的位置未能被完整记录。
【English】From 1831 to 1836, the team withdrew and scattered. The negative sequence shows Mallory pressing the Nikon S shutter multiple times while running, falling, or after being struck. The flash did not fire every time. Vance's position was not fully recorded.

【中文】1840，小队在返回 HZ-6A 前分散。克莱恩、基恩与万斯先后失去目视接触。拉斯克可能发射过一枚信号弹；两支 M1 卡宾枪是否开火未确认。
【English】At 1840, the team scattered before returning to HZ-6A. Klein, Keene, and Vance were successively lost from visual contact. Rusk may have fired one signal flare; whether the two M1 carbines were fired is unconfirmed.

【中文】1905，马洛里抵达黑湖支流附近浅水区。他记得水、手套里的碎闪光灯泡，以及自己反复推动过片杆，但无法说明为何离开采样区域。
【English】At 1905, Mallory reached shallow water near a Black Lake tributary. He remembered water, broken flashbulbs inside his gloves, and repeatedly advancing the film lever, but could not explain why he had left the sampling area.

【English】At 0218 on November 19, 1952, the rescue team found Mallory about 1.4 miles west of the planned return route. The Nikon S was still hanging from its neck strap, and the film had been advanced to the end. The Rolleiflex was missing.
