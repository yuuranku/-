# 白渊纪事：低清晰度胶片档案照 Prompt 版

使用方式：每次复制一整条 prompt 到网页 GPT 图像生成。这个版本的核心是“信息残缺”，不要追求精美照片。

## 通用压清晰度模块

如果你想改旧 prompt，可以直接把下面这段追加到每条末尾：

Make the final image look like a damaged, low-resolution archival scan of a mid-20th-century black-and-white gelatin silver print, not a restored high-definition photograph. The subject should be only partially legible. Reduce apparent sharpness across the whole frame. Use soft focus, slight lens misalignment, film grain clumping, scanning blur, chemical fog, silvering, dust, scratches, water stains, uneven development, crushed shadows, blown snow highlights, and low micro-contrast. Avoid crisp edges, modern clarity, high dynamic range, perfect focus, clean contrast, cinematic polish, digital sharpness, readable text, and clean documentary photography.

## 01 静态：1947 年钻探站外景，低清晰度版

Create a fictional 1947 Antarctic drilling-camp archival photograph related to the earliest White Abyss resource survey. The image is a black-and-white gelatin silver print found in a damaged government file. A squat rotary drilling tower stands half-buried in snow, with canvas tents, frost-covered fuel drums, cable reels, wooden crates, and a few bundled engineers nearby. The composition should feel static and official, but the actual details should be hard to inspect.

Photographic constraints: shot on an old 4x5 press camera in extreme cold, ASA 80 or ASA 100 black-and-white sheet film, tripod exposure, slight bellows or lens alignment issue, frost on the lens edge, weak winter sunlight reflected by snow. The center is only moderately readable; faces are indistinct; the drill tower edges are softened; snow highlights are blown out; shadows under equipment are crushed. Add archival scan damage: silvering along the borders, fine scratches, chemical fog, dust specks, uneven paper texture, soft scan blur. Do not make it crisp or cinematic. No modern equipment, no color, no readable text, no logo, no clean high-resolution restoration.

## 02 静态：1952 年冰下原始生态，低清晰度版

Create a 1952 black-and-white scientific field photograph from a White Abyss subglacial ecology survey. The scene is inside a newly opened under-ice cavern. The ecosystem should look primitive and ancient: low fern-like growth, horsetail-like segmented stems, wet mineral terraces, shallow black water, pale rootlike mats on stone, and ice walls catching harsh flash. It must not look like a modern forest, mushroom biome, fantasy jungle, or alien planet. Two researchers in heavy canvas parkas stand at the edge for scale, but their faces and equipment are barely readable.

Photographic constraints: medium-format Rolleiflex field camera, slow black-and-white film, magnesium flash, underexposed cavern background, center flash hotspot, heavy falloff into blackness, condensation on the lens, slight tripod vibration, poor field processing. The primitive plants should be visible only as soft silhouettes and coarse textures, not botanical catalog detail. Add dense grain, water stains, gray fogging, bent paper corners, dust, scratches, and low-resolution archival scan blur. The image should feel like evidence recovered from an old file, not a sharp nature documentary. No color, no readable labels, no modern lamps, no clean CGI, no highly detailed creature or plant rendering.

## 03 动态：1956 年冰下动物接触，严重糊片版

Create a damaged 1956 black-and-white field photograph taken during a sudden White Abyss animal encounter in a subglacial ravine. The photo should be a near-failed shot. Expedition members are turning lamps toward shallow water, and a large primitive amphibian-like animal is moving away through the water. The animal must not be clearly visible: only a low wet body shape, a suggestion of a long back, and maybe one blurred ridge should be readable.

Photographic constraints: handheld 35mm camera, Kodak Tri-X pushed hard, ISO 800 or ISO 1600 equivalent, shutter around 1/8 to 1/15 second, no tripod, photographer startled and moving. Use strong motion blur, lamp trails, diagonal camera shake, missed focus, partial flash bloom, water reflection smear, and a tilted composition. Faces, hands, and the animal should be ambiguous. Add clumped grain, frame-edge vignetting, overexposed flashlight streaks, crushed blacks, scratches, dust, and scanning softness. It should look like an accidental failed photograph whose value is in the anomaly, not in clarity. Avoid sharp creature design, heroic framing, modern horror movie lighting, digital clarity, readable text, or color.

## 04 静态：1959 年原始节肢动物标本，低清晰度版

Create a 1959 black-and-white laboratory archive photograph of a primitive arthropod specimen recovered from the White Abyss ecosystem. The specimen lies on a white enamel tray beside old calipers, glass vials, metal tweezers, and blank paper tags. It should look ancient, simple, and unsettling: segmented shell, short jointed limbs, damp membrane, but not a fantasy monster and not gore.

Photographic constraints: old 4x5 view camera with close-focus bellows, black-and-white sheet film, single weak overhead lamp, shallow depth of field, slight macro focusing error. Only part of the specimen is in soft focus; the limbs and tray edges fade into blur; the white tray is a little blown out; the tabletop is muddy gray. Add chemical stains, paper fibers, dust, scratches, silvering, and scan blur. The final image should feel like a poorly preserved lab print copied several times, with biological details partly lost. Do not make it crisp, glossy, or modern. No readable text, no color, no clean museum specimen photography, no high-resolution macro clarity.

## 05 动态：1961 年失踪队员最后一张，极低清晰度版

Create a 1961 black-and-white emergency photograph from a White Abyss underground survey tunnel, supposedly the last image before several expedition members disappeared. Three people in heavy polar clothing run toward the camera through ice dust and darkness. In the far background, a faint duplicated human shape suggests a personnel-overlap anomaly, but it should be almost impossible to confirm. The supernatural element must be hidden inside photographic failure.

Photographic constraints: handheld Leica-style 35mm camera, pushed high-speed black-and-white film, 1/8 second shutter or slower, flashbulb firing too close to the foreground, photographer running backward. Use severe motion blur, camera shake, flare, flash overexposure on coats, underexposed tunnel depth, smeared lamp trails, and missed focus. The duplicated figure should be a soft gray smear, not a clear ghost. Add heavy grain, scratches, torn negative edge, water damage, chemical fog, dust, and low-resolution scan compression softness. The image should be difficult to read, like an analog horror evidence frame. No clean faces, no sharp monster, no modern camera look, no color, no readable text.

## 06 静态：1953 年无线电室记忆错乱，模糊档案版

Create a 1953 black-and-white interior archive photograph of a cramped Antarctic radio room connected to a White Abyss memory-disorder incident. No people are visible. The room contains vacuum-tube radio equipment, old headphones, a frost-rimmed window, a metal chair, paper logs, two identical enamel mugs, and two nearly identical heavy parkas hanging in different parts of the room. The anomaly should be quiet and easy to miss.

Photographic constraints: medium-format camera on tripod, weak tungsten bulbs, slow film, long exposure, slight vibration from a generator, focus set slightly behind the desk. The room should not be sharp: radio knobs are soft, paper writing is unreadable, the window is blown out, corners are dark, and the duplicated objects are visible but not crisp. Add smoky gray shadows, film grain, chemical fog, dust, scratches, silvering, stains, and a soft low-resolution scan. Make it feel like a copied investigative print, not a clean set photograph. No readable text, no modern electronics, no color, no dramatic ghost, no digital sharpness.

## 07 静态：1960 年站点合影人员重叠，低清晰度版

Create a 1960 black-and-white formal group photograph outside an Antarctic White Abyss research station. Twelve personnel in heavy mid-century polar clothing stand stiffly in front of a low station entrance. One official-looking man appears twice in the group, with the same face and body type but different posture and slightly different age impression. The duplication must be subtle and made less certain by poor photographic quality.

Photographic constraints: large-format press camera, flashbulb, cold wind, snow glare, old ASA 100 film, slightly misfocused group portrait. Most faces should be soft and difficult to identify; the duplicated man should be believable but not perfectly clear. Add light double-exposure edge ghosting, gray fog, scratched print surface, uneven contrast, silvering at the edges, paper creases, dust, and low-resolution archival scan blur. Do not make it a sharp modern group photo. No readable sign text, no color, no modern clothing, no cinematic horror pose, no clean digital restoration.

## 08 动态：1962 年基地撤离公共危机，新闻糊片版

Create a 1962 black-and-white crisis photograph showing a rushed Antarctic base evacuation during a White Abyss incident. Old tracked snow vehicles pull canvas-covered cargo; personnel carry sealed metal crates; a radio mast stands in the background; windblown snow crosses the frame. The scene should be urgent but visually messy, like a military press photo taken in bad weather.

Photographic constraints: handheld 35mm camera, Tri-X pushed to ISO 1600, shutter around 1/30 second while panning, snow blowing into the lens, poor exposure control. Moving people and vehicles should be blurred; only parts of the radio mast and base silhouette are readable. Use grain clumping, snow streaks, motion smear, overexposed white flare from snow, crushed black coats, crooked framing, scratches, dust, and scan softness. The image should not look clean or heroic. Avoid modern vehicles, helicopters, readable markings, color, crisp action photography, glossy war-movie composition, or digital clarity.

## 09 静态：1958 年冰壁站点重叠影像，低对比版

Create a 1958 black-and-white survey photograph inside a White Abyss subglacial ice tunnel. The foreground contains an old metal survey tripod, a level instrument, cables, and acetylene or early battery lamps. The main subject is a smooth deep ice wall where faint human silhouettes seem embedded or reflected at impossible depth, as if a station scene from another time has been weakly recorded in the ice. The anomaly must be very low contrast and uncertain.

Photographic constraints: 4x5 large-format camera, slow ASA 64 film, tripod long exposure, cold condensation on lens, lamps causing halation. The tripod can be moderately readable, but the ice-wall figures should be almost lost in gray fog and internal reflection. Add soft focus, low micro-contrast, long-exposure haze, lamp bloom, dense shadow grain, scratches, dust, uneven chemical development, and archival scan blur. It should look like analysts would argue about what they are seeing. No clear ghosts, no horror faces, no modern instruments, no color, no readable text, no sharp ice-cave photography.

## 10 动态：1964 年冰下动物迁徙，车载失败照版

Create a 1964 black-and-white night photograph taken from a moving snowcat or underground expedition vehicle near a White Abyss access route. Searchlights catch a line of primitive long-legged animals crossing an underground frozen plain. The animals should be distant and indistinct, visible mostly as smeared silhouettes and leg streaks. A partial vehicle frame, a gloved hand, or a researcher shoulder may appear in the foreground, but all details should feel accidental.

Photographic constraints: handheld 35mm rangefinder, pushed black-and-white film, ISO 1600 equivalent, 1/10 second shutter from a vibrating moving vehicle, dirty window glass, weak searchlights, bad framing. Use strong directional motion blur, light streaks, double images on the animals, soft foreground obstruction, grain clumping, frame-edge vignetting, slight light leak, scratches, dust, and low-resolution scan softness. The photograph should look like a failed proof of a major discovery, not a clear wildlife image. No sharp animal anatomy, no modern vehicle interior, no color, no cinematic lighting, no readable text, no polished concept art.
