# HZ-6/R06 Nikon F non-black frame prompt pack

This pack covers the non-black useful and semi-useful Nikon F frames from the HZ-6/R06 roll. Fully black frames are intentionally skipped. "Waste frames" are included when motion blur, flash failure, water reflection, edge damage, or accidental framing still preserves a clue.

Shared identity:
- Event: HZ-6 Sample Line Field Record
- Agency: Bureau of Antarctic Survey
- Photographer: Thomas E. Mallory
- Date: November 18, 1961
- Camera: Nikon F 35mm SLR
- Film: Kodak Tri-X Pan ASA 400
- Format: horizontal 35mm frame, archival black-and-white gelatin silver print scan
- Location: HZ-6 Sample Line, twilight conifer layer beneath Antarctic ice

Shared low-clarity rule:
Every image must look like a damaged, low-resolution mid-century black-and-white archival scan, not a restored photo. Use soft focus, lens misalignment, clumped Tri-X grain, scanning blur, chemical fog, silvering, dust, scratches, water stains, uneven development, crushed shadows, blown frost highlights, pressure marks, and low micro-contrast. No color, no modern clarity, no readable text, no logos, no modern gear, no gore, no clear monster design.
