# HZ-6 五人入职档案照与出任务前合照提示词

用途：复制到网页端 GPT / GPT Image，用来生成 HZ-6 样本线任务五名成员的人员档案照，以及一张出任务前合照。

这次需要把两类照片分开：

- 五张大头照是“入职、借调、转入项目、人员建档时”的档案照，不是 HZ-6 出发前同一天拍的。
- 合照才是 1961 年 11 月 18 日 HZ-6 出任务前，在地平线站装备间留下的任务记录照片。
- 五个人不要穿得一模一样，也不要像同一个模特换脸。年龄、体型、身份、服装、拍摄地点、建档年份都要有差别。
- 合照不要排成五个人肩并肩的标准证件合影，要像真实任务前的装备清点记录：有人站着看路线，有人检查无线电，有人坐在样本箱旁，有人半转身整理相机，有人在门口或后方警戒。

## 通用老照片质感

每张都可以附加这一段：

```text
Generate a fictional mid-20th-century black-and-white archival photograph. This is not a modern portrait, not a cinematic still, not a restored vintage filter. It must look like a gelatin silver print scanned from an old personnel file: soft optical resolution, low micro-contrast, slightly uneven exposure, visible silver grain, dust, small scratches, faint paper texture, archive handling marks, mild edge wear, and imperfect development. No color. No modern digital sharpness. No glossy fashion lighting. No readable text, no labels, no watermark, no logo, no modern ID card, no modern tactical gear, no contemporary haircut or fashion.

The photograph should feel practical, bureaucratic, and documentary. Use plain institutional lighting, weak flash, office light, equipment-room light, or military personnel-office light. Faces may be visible in the personnel file photos, but they should look natural, tired, and unglamorous. Avoid heroic poses, dramatic horror lighting, and movie-poster composition.
```

```text
Important anti-cloning instruction: treat the five team members as five different real people from different departments, not as variants of one model. Rusk is older, tallest, broadest, square military bearing. Klein is the only woman, shorter, compact, bespectacled, scientific. Keene is slim, narrow-faced, nervous, technical radio operator. Mallory is civilian, medium build, withdrawn, camera equipment, not military. Vance is stocky, broad-shouldered, guarded, shorter than Rusk, rear-security Navy man. Give each person different posture, different clothing details, different head shape, different body type, and a different task in the room.
```
